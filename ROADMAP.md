# Roadmap

CM0304 Save Editor focuses on two workflows for **CM0304 4.1.5**. Use the same player, staff and club profiles to research a career or stage changes and save a validated copy.

## Delivered in v1.1

- Browse and filter players, staff and clubs. Keep saved searches and watchlists, compare profiles and export results.
- Edit supported fields, preview bulk operations and review derived effects. Save and reopen plans linked to the source file by SHA-256.
- Keep the active tab and scroll position after staging a change. Search the review and jump back to the affected field.
- Find the destination and copy history after a write. The Windows x64 release publishes a signed updater package.

## Scouting and navigation

- Combine multiple attribute criteria in one search and preserve filters, selection and pagination when returning from a profile.
- Compare a positional Scout Rating with CM Scout 3.10 on the same save before using it for ranking or filtering.
- Expose recognised person and club relationships in profiles, with navigation to the related entity.
- Investigate shortlist interchange with the game and verify both import and export in CM0304 4.1.5.

## Editing and compatibility

- Improve shortcuts for checking a club's squad and staff and previewing supported fitness or morale changes.
- Investigate extending each contract from its current expiry, including rolling contracts and leap days.
- Test more careers and save variants. Prioritise problems affecting source integrity, entity selection and loading edited copies.
- Keep experimental transfer operations in the research CLI until squad, ownership, registration and other references have been verified in the game.
